# EcoWrap

A responsive storefront concept for custom-branded, eco-friendly packaging: takeaway bags, tissue paper, and made-to-order boxes.

## Preview locally

Open `index.html` in a browser. The page uses Google Fonts and product photography hosted by Unsplash, so an internet connection is needed for those assets.

## Custom quote form

The form validates contact details and prepares an enquiry in the visitor's default email app. Replace `hello@ecowrap.com` in `index.html` with your real business email before publishing. The logo upload is previewed in the browser; it is not uploaded to a server. To receive logo files directly, connect the form to a backend or a form service.
